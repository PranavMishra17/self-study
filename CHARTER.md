# Charter

Written 14 September 2026. This file does not get rewritten.

Everything else in this repo is provisional. Milestones get re-planned, weeks get
missed, tracks get reordered. This page is the thing all of that gets checked
against. When a re-plan contradicts something here, that is not automatically
wrong, but it must be written into `CHANGELOG.md` as a deviation with a reason.
Drift with a record is adaptation. Drift without one is what happened last year.

---

## What this is for

To stop being the engineer who ships well and cannot explain why anything works.
By the end of 2027, to walk into any serious AI company in India or the US and be
qualified in fact, not just in interest.

## What the gap actually is

Not intelligence, not confidence, and not work ethic — the shipping record settles
all three. It is that a year of building production systems rewards knowing *what*
works and never once forces you to derive *why*. The mathematics went unused and
went quiet. The systems vocabulary was never needed because the systems were small
enough to hold in your head. Neither is a flaw in how you think; both are the
predictable shape of a career spent shipping rather than studying, and both are
recoverable by the ordinary method of sitting down with them.

The 14 September diagnostic is treated here as exactly one thing: a calibration
reading that says where to start. Taken on a busy day, it maps which topics need
ground-up work and which only need dusting. That is all it is evidence of.

## Five commitments

1. **Mathematics gets touched every week, permanently.** It is the only track with
   no external forcing function, which is exactly why it is the one that silently
   disappears. Nobody will ever interview on derivatives. It compounds anyway.

2. **One primary track at a time.** Two and a half hours a week split three ways is
   how four months pass having touched everything and learned nothing. Depth over
   coverage, always.

3. **Interruptions are expected and planned for.** A Red week of ninety minutes
   counts as an unbroken week. An interview sprint is a Red week, not the end of the
   plan. There is a defined way back in and it gets used.

4. **No back-filling, ever.** Missed hours are never owed. Accumulated debt is the
   most common reason long plans get dropped, so this one never creates any.

5. **Artifacts over credentials.** The measure is things that exist and can be
   defended in a room, not courses completed.

## What is explicitly not the goal

- Finishing books. DDIA is a reference hung off topics, not a reading schedule.
- Covering a syllabus. Parked material stays parked without guilt.
- Optimising for one interview. The plan survives a callback; it does not reshape
  itself around one.

## The honest floor

Four to six hours a week. Roughly one to two hundred hours before this reaches
India. It is enough only if it is never negotiated downward mid-week and never
spent on three things at once.
